What is CareerSpace?

Your Personal Placement Management Platform

CareerSpace is your personal placement management platform designed to simplify every step of your placement journey.

Instead of juggling spreadsheets, notes, and multiple resume files, CareerSpace helps you organise everything in one secure and structured workspace.

Self-Driven & Student-Centric

Designed specifically to empower students without third-party corporate clutter.

All-In-One Placement Hub

Keep applications, custom resumes, notes, and peer interview wisdom neatly linked.
